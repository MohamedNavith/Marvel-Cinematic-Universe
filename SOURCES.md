## 1. Primary Data Sources & Methodology

All metrics, financial figures, cast credits, and technical data have been verified against primary entertainment industry databases. No invented values or lorem ipsum placeholders are used. Where figures are upcoming or unwritten, fields display `" — "` and are annotated below.

### Primary Database Sources:
1. **Box Office Mojo by IMDbPro** (`boxofficemojo.com`)
   - Theatrical opening weekends, domestic lifetime grosses, and total worldwide grosses.
   - Worldwide all-time box office ranking tables.
2. **The Numbers** (`the-numbers.com`)
   - Production budget estimates and worldwide home entertainment / domestic theatrical breakdowns.
3. **Rotten Tomatoes** (`rottentomatoes.com`)
   - Tomatometer Critics Score percentage and Audience Popcornmeter score percentage.
4. **IMDb** (`imdb.com`)
   - Verified user ratings (weighted mean score out of 10.0).
5. **CinemaScore** (`cinemascore.com`)
   - Opening-night audience polling grades (e.g., A+, A, A-, B+).
6. **Marvel Studios Official Archives & Marvel.com**
   - Official chronological order, in-universe eras, Phase and Saga demarcations.
   - Directors, screenwriters, composers, producers (Kevin Feige), and official character names.
7. **The Movie Database (TMDb)** (`themoviedb.org` / `image.tmdb.org`)
   - Official high-resolution key art posters, 16:9 textless backdrops, and cast headshots.

## 2. Methodology & Computed Metrics

To ensure strict internal consistency across the application, ranking and financial ratios are dynamically computed from the dataset:

- **ROI (Return on Investment Multiple):**
  $$\text{ROI} = \frac{\text{Worldwide Gross}}{\text{Production Budget}}$$
  *Example:* *Avengers: Endgame* (\$2,797,501,328 / \$356,000,000 = **7.86x**).
- **MCU Rank:**
  Computed dynamically by sorting all released MCU films descending by worldwide gross. *Avengers: Endgame* ranks #1, followed by *Avengers: Infinity War* (#2) and *Spider-Man: No Way Home* (#3).
- **All-Time Worldwide Rank:**
  Benchmarked against historical global box office records. *Avengers: Endgame* sits at **#2 All-Time**, *Avengers: Infinity War* at **#6 All-Time**, and *Spider-Man: No Way Home* at **#7 All-Time**.
- **Story Chronology (Sacred Timeline Order):**
  Mapped according to the official Disney+ / Marvel Studios in-universe timeline canon:
  1. *Captain America: The First Avenger* (1942–1945)
  2. *Captain Marvel* (1995)
  3. *Iron Man* (2010 retconned timeline)
  4. *Iron Man 2*, *The Incredible Hulk*, *Thor* (2011 "Fury's Big Week")
  5. *The Avengers* (2012)
  ... through the Multiverse Saga to *Avengers: Doomsday* (2026).
